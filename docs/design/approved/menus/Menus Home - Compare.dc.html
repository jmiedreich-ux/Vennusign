What the choice actually decides
1a survives being a single venue better. Strip the venue line and it is M1 today — no layout change, nothing to hide. 1b at one venue is a table with one meaningful column.
1b survives scale better. Thirteen menus is three rows of cards you scroll past, or thirteen lines you scan in one screenful. Corporate opens this screen to find what is wrong, not to admire boards.
1a keeps import and the empty state honest. The shelf's empty state is the onboarding flow — decision 17. A table's empty state is an empty table.
1b names names. “Southbank 3 days, Pier 9 9 days” is the sentence corporate acts on. On a card it either truncates or pushes the card taller than its thumbnail.
