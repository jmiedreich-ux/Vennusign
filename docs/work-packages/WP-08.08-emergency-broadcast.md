## Goal

Add bounded venue-wide or screen-targeted full-screen broadcasts with realtime activation/cancellation and deterministic expiry recovery.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A broadcast targets either every venue screen or one screen proven to belong to the venue.
- Titles/messages are bounded and duration is 1–1440 minutes with authoritative UTC expiry.
- Active selection ignores cancelled/expired rows and gives targeted broadcasts precedence.
- Protected tier-visible administration can activate and cancel broadcasts.
- Realtime events patch broadcast state without replacing cached menu/playlist content.
- The full-screen player overlay expires locally from authoritative UTC and restores underlying playback.
- Required non-integration Actions checks pass; integration-type tests remain skipped.
