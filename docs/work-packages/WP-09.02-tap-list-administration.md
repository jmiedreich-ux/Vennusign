## Goal

Add protected, venue-scoped, tier-visible administration for tap categories and beer-specific tap items.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Categories and items support list, create, edit, delete, and exact reorder operations.
- Category ownership is verified within the venue and in-use categories cannot be deleted.
- Names, prices, ABV, IBU, description, and colors are bounded and normalized.
- Availability and coming-soon controls are independently editable.
- All controls stay visible but soft locked without All Layouts.
- Changes publish through the established venue content notification path.
- Required non-integration Actions checks pass; integration tests remain skipped.

## Boundaries

No tap-board layout rendering, pairing changes, POS behavior, or integration infrastructure.
