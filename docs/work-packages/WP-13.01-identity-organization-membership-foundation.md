## Goal

Establish the persisted customer identity and organization-tenancy foundation required by every later approved Phase 13 package.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Customer users and external identity links have explicit normalized uniqueness without adding a password-login model.
- Organizations have one active owner, explicit organization memberships, and audited ownership transfer.
- Existing venues can be attached to one owning organization, and venue memberships cannot cross that organization boundary.
- Organization and venue roles resolve through deterministic server-owned capability definitions; unknown roles and capabilities are denied.
- Membership creation, role change, revoke, and ownership-transfer actions record immutable actor, subject, target, action, and UTC audit evidence.
- Repository and service contracts are cancellation-aware and organization/venue scoped.
- DbUp migration, dependency registration, focused repository/service/migration tests, and architecture records are included.
- Required affected-area GitHub Actions checks pass on the exact reviewed head.

## Boundaries

- No password, OAuth/OIDC callback, session cookie, passkey, TOTP, recovery, trial, Stripe entitlement, onboarding state, or UI behavior.
- No replacement or removal of legacy `VenueAdmin:Sessions`; WP-13.09 owns that migration.
- No Phase 14 or later planning or implementation.
- Integration, Azure SQL, external-provider, hosted-infrastructure, container, physical-device, and cross-system tests remain skipped under the standing owner exception.

## Implementation

- Added customer users and provider-subject identity links with normalized email/provider uniqueness and no password credential model.
- Added organizations, organization memberships, venue ownership, venue memberships, deterministic capability resolution, and fail-closed role validation.
- Added atomic organization/member/venue mutation contracts whose state and immutable audit evidence share one SQL transaction.
- Added serialized ownership transfer, active-owner uniqueness, and composite tenant foreign keys that reject cross-organization venue memberships.
- Added migration 040, RepoDb mappings, dependency injection, focused repository/service/capability tests, migration inventory coverage, and the Phase 13 tenancy architecture record.
