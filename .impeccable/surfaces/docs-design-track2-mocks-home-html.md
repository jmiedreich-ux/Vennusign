# Track 2 redesign mocks — Back Office (Home, Screens, Menu builder)

Scope: static design mocks for owner review at `docs/design/track2-mocks/`. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the venue owner in short interrupted bursts (phone or laptop at the venue), with occasional longer desk sessions. Job: see what the TVs are showing, change content, know it landed, understand every refusal.

Chosen direction: **The Instrument Bench** — the nixie-laboratory-counter world (user-chosen challenger, seed 519d0cc8) fused with the binding sky identity. Quantities are the interface; every change is a visible physical event. The dark lives only inside instrument glass (display windows, digit banks); the page world is light: white enamel panels on pale sky ground, slate hairlines, engraved plate labels, sky-blue lit digits and lamps, semantic status colors as physical lamps.

Signature moves:
- Paired AUTH/APPLIED revision counters per TV — counter agreement is the product's "applied" claim made physical.
- Three kinds of "no" as instrument states: permission = locked plate; not-in-plan = unfitted slot with blanking plate; temporarily blocked = amber maintenance lamp with return time.
- Destructive actions behind a guard cover; the confirm step is the guard.
- Home is the master panel: NOW SHOWING TV windows, counters, annunciator (NEEDS YOU), event register strip.
- Menu edits accumulate on a visible change ticket; SEND rolls the revision counter and each TV's applied counter chases it.

Memorable moment: the amber annunciator lamp pulsing over a TV that needs attention, and two revision counters clicking into agreement after a send.

Unresolved: whether the register strip carries into Schedules; whether the ticket batches per-menu or per-venue; DESIGN.md deliberately deferred until the owner approves the direction for implementation.
