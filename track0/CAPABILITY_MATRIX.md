## Matrix rules

- Do not create a new row merely to restate inherited Restaurant behavior.
- One row must have exactly one primary classification.
- Tier, add-on, limit, permission, and state columns describe relationships; they do not replace the primary classification.
- Candidate packaging remains unapproved until all native industries are normalized and the owner approves it.
