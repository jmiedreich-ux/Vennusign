## Coherence and inheritance

Restaurant remains the canonical baseline. Entertainment inherits general organization, venue, content, screen, targeting, publishing, delivery, version, user, role, permission, schedule, notice, localization, accessibility, analytics, and integration concepts.

The profile adds only meaningful Entertainment deltas: venue and experience hierarchies; attractions, exhibits, programs, events, sessions, queues, routes, gates and sections; timed and continuous occurrences; doors, starts, endings, last entry, boarding, seating and check-in; availability, delay, closure, cancellation, relocation, restriction, weather and reopening states; queue, wait, capacity and admission guidance; visitor wayfinding; and ticketing, admissions, access, venue, event, sports, map, rights-holder and managed-service boundaries.

The subtype model is bounded, has a neutral fallback, and resolves hybrids with one primary subtype plus descriptive traits. Subtype changes terminology, starter suggestions, screen-purpose recommendations, operating prompts, dashboard emphasis, and analytics presentation only. It does not grant permissions, tiers, add-ons, limits, privacy rights, or rollout access.

The terminology model consistently distinguishes venue, area, program, experience, event, session, occurrence, schedule, admission, ticket, pass, membership, reservation, timed entry, credential, queue, wait, capacity, notice, route, screen, publish, delivery, and restore. Public wording remains distinct from operator-only source, freshness, authority, target, and recovery detail.

## Operating model

The combined operating model covers venue operating days, local time zones, timed occurrences, continuous experiences, doors, last entry, boarding, seating, check-in, closures, delays, pauses, cancellations, relocations, restrictions, weather effects, reopening, event surges, queue/wait/capacity/admission guidance, wayfinding, accessibility, languages, sources, overrides, offline screens, partial delivery, outages, and restoration.

High-scope communication requires explicit authority, scope, audience, language, effective time, target preview, delivery visibility, correction, expiration, supersession, and recovery. Track 0 does not invent legal, life-safety, accessibility-compliance, rights, licensing, gambling, alcohol, child-safety, biometric, camera, or jurisdiction-specific policy.

## Essential core

Every Entertainment & Attractions customer retains these capabilities without a premium tier or paid integration:

1. Maintain basic venue, area, experience, schedule, occurrence, route, and visitor-information context.
2. Manually communicate available, limited, full, sold out, delayed, paused, closed, canceled, relocated, restricted, weather-affected, reopening, and unknown states with explicit scope.
3. Manually provide queue, wait, capacity, admission, boarding, seating, check-in, last-entry, wayfinding, route, notice, language, accessibility, and next-action guidance.
4. Pair or select screens, choose purpose and context, and deliberately defer incomplete setup with a saved next action.
5. Select exact targets, preview public wording, language, effective time, source/freshness, and replaced content.
6. Publish and see accepted, pending, delivered, partial, failed, offline, outdated, incompatible, excluded, and unknown state by target.
7. Correct, supersede, unpublish, retry, undo where safe, and restore a prior successful version.
8. Continue manual operation when any external source is absent, disconnected, stale, conflicting, or unsupported.
9. Understand whether an action is blocked by permission, tier, add-on, limit, privacy, source, configuration, compatibility, or rollout.
10. Receive first-use, empty, validation, permission, stale, conflict, offline, partial, failure, success, and recovery guidance.

No ordinary essential operation depends on ticketing, admissions, access control, queue measurement, sensors, mapping, venue management, show control, collection management, attraction management, event or sports systems, CRM, POS, AI, advanced analytics, or managed hardware/service.

## Classification and packaging

Each concern has one primary classification: core capability, product/domain state, permission, tier candidate, independent add-on, limit, or rollout flag. These conditions must never be collapsed into a generic “unavailable” feature flag.

The working Operate, Coordinate, Portfolio, and Enterprise archetypes form a coherent outcome progression. Operate preserves the complete required manual baseline. Coordinate adds native workflow and coordination. Portfolio adds multi-venue standards and governance while preserving local control. Enterprise adds enterprise administration, audit, retention, identity/governance workflow, and service management.

External systems and managed services remain independent add-ons. Counts and consumption remain limits. Final names, pricing, trials, contracts, discounts, exact entitlements, numeric limits, providers, regions, checkout, billing, and implementation sequencing remain owner decisions.

## Customer journeys and analytics integrity

Onboarding reaches one verified active screen before forced pricing, a complete venue model, or a paid integration. The first-value loop is coherent: establish venue context, choose one visitor outcome, pair or select a screen, create accurate content, perform one real update, preview, publish, confirm per-target delivery, and land on an exception-first starter menu. RWP-13.06 remains paused.

The dashboard continues the same operating model: context and public-impact exceptions first, then Quick Update, now/today/next, schedules, queues/capacity/admission, wayfinding, notices, source/freshness, screen/publication health, upcoming work, and authorized multi-venue oversight.

Core analytics use Vennusign-owned screen, content, publication, delivery, notice, schedule, version, source, freshness, and recovery records. The model correctly separates publish request, accepted publication, confirmed delivery, visitor measurement, attendance, conversion, and revenue. External or measured data requires an authoritative add-on source and approved privacy, rights, retention, and permission model.

The combined profile applies Impeccable outcome-led onboarding, Operate, shape, clarify, harden, and bounded polish guidance, including keyboard and assistive-technology access, 200% zoom, localization expansion, right-to-left readiness, long names, mobile through desktop behavior, non-color-only feedback, safe high-scope confirmation, and complete failure and recovery states.
