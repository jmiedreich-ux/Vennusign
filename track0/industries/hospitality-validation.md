## Validation result

### Restaurant inheritance

Hospitality correctly inherits the Restaurant baseline for embedded food-and-beverage venues while adding property, accommodation, amenity, service, event, meeting, wayfinding, language, source, delivery, and multi-property operating context. Hospitality does not redefine inherited Restaurant behavior merely to create a new package.

### Industry and subtype coherence

The profile consistently treats Hospitality and its primary subtype as product configuration that tunes terminology, starter objects, guidance, and presentation. Industry and subtype do not create permissions, entitlements, add-ons, limits, privacy rights, source authority, or rollout state.

The approved subtype set and neutral fallback cover Hotel, Resort, Motel, Hostel, Extended-Stay, Serviced Apartment, Conference Property, Casino Resort, Boutique Lodging, and mixed/uncertain Hospitality operation without forcing unsupported specialization.

### Terminology and operating model

The terminology model is consistent across property, building, tower, wing, floor, area, venue, outlet, amenity, service, event, meeting space, destination, notice, schedule, source, state, and screen concepts. Customer-authored names are preserved. Public wording remains privacy-safe.

The operating model correctly separates continuous property operation from the independent state and schedule of each desk, amenity, service, outlet, event, route, area, and screen. Overnight date boundaries, shifts and handoffs, arrival/stay/departure rhythms, event peaks, changed routes, urgent notices, multilingual content, property groups, source freshness, and recovery are covered.

### Required core protection

The profile consistently keeps essential daily operation core:

- manual property and guest information;
- hours and operating states;
- amenities, services, and outlet communication;
- meetings, events, and directories;
- manual wayfinding and temporary routes;
- notices and authorized urgent communication;
- basic manually authored language variants;
- explicit targeting and preview;
- save, schedule, publish, and confirmed delivery;
- offline, outdated, stale-source, conflict, and override awareness;
- correction, expiry, supersession, retry, undo, and restoration;
- current exceptions and shift handoff visibility;
- required accessibility, responsive, empty, loading, failure, partial-delivery, success, and recovery states.

No required capability is reclassified as premium or made dependent on a paid integration.

### Classification integrity

Every concern has one primary Track 0 classification:

- required Vennusign operating behavior: core capability;
- user authority: permission;
- represented business/system values: product/domain state;
- advanced Vennusign workflow, coordination, governance, localization, analytics, and enterprise administration: tier-entitlement candidate;
- external synchronization, consumption-backed services, identity connections, managed hardware, connectivity, monitoring, and related services: independent add-on candidate;
- counts, retention, volume, frequency, storage, data, and spend: usage or quantity limit;
- experiments, migrations, compatibility controls, staged release, and emergency disable: internal rollout flag.

Mixed concerns such as personalization, advanced wayfinding, localization/AI, analytics, enterprise identity, and managed service have explicit tier/add-on separation.

### Tier and add-on boundaries

The proposed Operate, Coordinate, Portfolio, and Enterprise outcome archetypes preserve all core operation in Operate. Higher tiers add coordination, governance, scale, and insight. External and managed services remain independently selectable add-ons. Limits remain separate.

The proposal does not approve final tier names, prices, numeric limits, trials, contracts, grandfathering, or implementation. Those remain owner decisions.

### Customer journey coherence

Onboarding progresses from property/subtype identification to minimal public context, starter content, one screen, exact preview, publication, confirmed delivery, and a task-first starter menu. It does not force tier selection or integration before the first active screen. Deferred questions, save/resume, pairing interruption, partial delivery, stale sources, correction, and recovery are explicit.

The dashboard continues the onboarding outcome with attention-first exceptions, current guest communication, task-first actions, property status, notices, amenities/outlets, meetings/events, wayfinding, screen/publication health, source health, and recovery. Role and shift tune presentation without granting capability or authority.

### Analytics integrity

Core analytics remain operational truth: screen, delivery, notice, object, language, source, event, route, and recovery health. Advanced trends, comparison, scheduled reporting, forecasting, optimization, and recommendations remain tier candidates. External PMS, event, occupancy, POS, sensor, access, transport, survey, loyalty, and related data remain add-on dependent.

Unknown, unavailable, stale, disconnected, partial, conflicting, restricted, not configured, not applicable, and zero remain distinct. Metrics require source, authority, formula, scope, time basis, freshness, coverage, exclusions, corrections, privacy, permission, retention, export, and reconciliation.

### Privacy, source, and safety

Public screens do not expose guest identity, room assignment, reservation, payment, loyalty, access, request, itinerary, or identifiable behavior by default. The profile does not infer room readiness, eligibility, access, capacity, wait, quantity, route, current position, accessibility, translation quality, source freshness, attendance, demand, revenue, satisfaction, attribution, or causation without authoritative data.

External sources require explicit authority, freshness, conflict behavior, privacy, scope, fallback, disconnect, retention, export, deletion, and recovery. Manual operation remains available when an integration is absent or failing.

### Accessibility and responsive completeness

The profile consistently requires keyboard and assistive-technology operation, visible focus, non-color-only state, 200% zoom, long-name support, localization expansion, right-to-left readiness, local dates and times, overnight clarity, reduced motion, and phone-through-large-desktop layouts. The approved Sky Blue administrative direction is preserved without using decorative style to replace task clarity.

## Unresolved owner decisions

- exact placement of advanced capabilities across tiers;
- numeric limits, pooling, overage, retention, refresh, and storage;
- pricing, trials, grandfathering, contract, and upgrade timing;
- add-on prerequisites and administration requirements;
- property-group inheritance, mandatory content, local override, and pooled-limit policy;
- downgrade grace, read-only behavior, conversion, export, retention, deletion, and active-content protection;
- enterprise identity and managed-service commercial structure;
- guest-specific personalization purpose and privacy model;
- analytics definitions requiring external data;
- emergency, safety, legal, security, and compliance obligations;
- implementation packages and sequencing after owner approval.

## Completion conclusion

Hospitality Track 0 is coherent, internally consistent, privacy-safe at the planning level, and ready for cross-industry normalization after the other approved industry streams complete. RWP-00.51 through RWP-00.62 form the authoritative Hospitality planning profile once this RWP is merged.

Do not start consolidation from this RWP. Consolidation begins only when the approved completion gate for all five native industries is satisfied.
