## Capability-classification validation

Every concern has one primary Track 0 classification.

### Core capability

Manual property and guest-communication workflows required for ordinary operation: information, notices, hours, states, events, directories, wayfinding, languages, targeting, preview, publishing, delivery confidence, source health, correction, and recovery.

### Product/domain state

Represented values such as industry, subtype, traits, property hierarchy, public names, object states, schedules, hours, notices, events, destinations, routes, language variants, source, freshness, override, targets, publication state, delivery state, and content version.

### Permission

Who may view, edit, approve, publish, restore, configure, compare, export, administer, purchase, or delete within authorized property and information scope. Plan access does not imply object authority or private-data access.

### Tier-entitlement candidate

Advanced native Vennusign outcomes such as shift workflow, campaigns, recurring schedules, advanced approvals, brand libraries, advanced wayfinding, advanced localization, property-group coordination, portfolio governance, analytics, enterprise administration, extended history, and scheduled reporting.

### Independent add-on candidate

External systems or managed services that can be selected independently: PMS/CRS, event/room-booking, registration, transport, parking, access, guest-service, spa, gaming, restaurant/POS, maps/positioning, translation, AI, analytics sources, identity providers, hardware, connectivity, monitoring, support, professional services, and custom integrations.

### Limit

Counts or consumption such as properties, buildings, venues, objects, screens, users, roles, languages, templates, campaigns, sources, integrations, history, storage, rows, refreshes, exports, reports, characters, tokens, support hours, incidents, replacements, and spend.

### Rollout flag

Internal temporary delivery control only. It must not be presented as a purchased capability, permission, product state, add-on, or quantity.

No classification collision or unassigned concern remains.
