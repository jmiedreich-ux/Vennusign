## Impeccable Planning Result

Future Food Truck & Concession Operate surfaces must be task-first and mobile-capable. Before service they prioritize setup readiness, location/event, menu, screen health, and publish confirmation. During service they prioritize Quick Update, sell-outs, service state, pickup/queue guidance, and recovery. Disruption flows show affected scope, authoritative state, guest message, publish result, and next action. Multi-unit views surface exceptions and mixed state before bulk actions.

Required planning states include first use, empty, permission, validation, offline, outdated, delivery failure, source conflict, success, undo/restoration, long names, 200% zoom, keyboard and assistive-technology access, non-color-only status, and confirmation for high-scope changes. Preserve the approved Sky Blue administrative direction.

No UI, API, schema, migration, billing, entitlement, feature-gate, route, order, payment, inventory, event, host, queue, analytics, or integration implementation was authorized or performed.
